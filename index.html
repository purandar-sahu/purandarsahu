Who I am & how I work

My name is Purandar Sahu, and in my local community I am widely known as Pinku. I belong to the Paikmal region of Bargarh district, Odisha. From an early stage, I chose not to wait around for opportunities to hand themselves to me—instead, I started combining practical digital skills with real-world service work to stand on my own feet.

Today, my daily routine revolves around five interconnected areas. First, I run Pinku Digital Point, my cyber cafe and digital service center, which serves as my primary source of income and ground-level business training. Helping dozens of people every week with online job portals, government certificates, scholarships, banking assistance, and documentation has taught me customer handling, accuracy under pressure, and financial responsibility.

Second, I work as a Frontend Developer—designing and deploying clean, responsive websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare. Alongside web development, I am actively learning Cyber Security fundamentals—understanding how web applications, networks, authentication systems, and online data stay protected against common vulnerabilities.

On the academic front, after scoring 77.17% (463/600) in my Class 10 board exams, I faced a setback in +2 where three subjects remained unclear. Rather than letting that pause my progress, I took fresh admission in NIOS Senior Secondary (Class 12) so I can complete my formal education cleanly while continuing to grow my technical skills and business operations toward my long-term goal of launching a registered Private Limited (Pvt. Ltd.) company.

My Work & Earning Engine

Pinku Digital Point is more than just a name—it is my self-built earning platform where I handle real cyber cafe and digital service operations every day. For many students, job seekers, farmers, and families in rural and semi-urban areas, navigating complex online portals, document upload specifications, payment gateways, and official forms can be stressful and prone to costly errors.

At Pinku Digital Point, every application is checked carefully before submission. Whether someone needs to apply for a competitive recruitment exam, update their PAN card, download an e-District certificate, register a small business under Udyam or FSSAI, or get urgent printouts and scanned PDFs prepared to exact KB dimensions, I ensure clear communication, upfront service charges, and reliable follow-up.
