// 03. ACADEMIC TIMELINE (YEAR-WISE)

Education journey & resilience

A transparent year-by-year record of my schooling—from primary classrooms to completing Senior Secondary through NIOS while working independently.

2026 – 2027 (Ongoing) Senior Secondary · Class 12

National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) — Stream 1, Block 1

After facing an academic setback in my regular +2 board exams, I took a practical decision to restart cleanly by enrolling in NIOS Senior Secondary through fresh admission based on my Class 10 qualification. This flexible open-schooling structure allows me to study systematically, prepare subject by subject, and complete my Class 12 qualification while simultaneously running Pinku Digital Point and building my web development skills.

2024 – 2026 (2 Years) Higher Secondary (+2 Arts)

Vindhya Vasini Higher Secondary School, Paikmal

Spent two years pursuing Higher Secondary (+2) education in the Arts/Humanities stream. Scored 265 marks overall, with three subjects remaining unclear. Rather than treating that phase as a defeat, I took full accountability for time management and used the experience as a turning point to become more disciplined in both academics and work.

2022 – 2024 (2 Years) Secondary · Classes 9 & 10

Panchayat High School, Kechhodadar

Completed two years of secondary schooling (Classes 9 and 10) and appeared for the BSE Odisha Class 10 Board Examination in 2024. Passed with 463 out of 600 marks (77.17%), building a strong academic base and sharpening my interest in computers, internet tools, and digital systems.

2014 – 2022 (8 Years) Primary & Upper Primary · Classes 1 to 8

Government U.P. School, Frezor Pur

Completed eight foundational years of schooling from Class 1 through Class 8. These early years shaped my core reading, writing, mathematics, and curiosity about how technology and everyday systems work.
